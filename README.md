# Nexora – Omnichannel Business Automation Platform

**Nexora** is a modern, multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform designed to help businesses manage customer interactions, sales, marketing, and business operations from a centralized dashboard.

The platform aims to simplify business management by integrating multiple communication channels, customer relationship management (CRM), AI-powered automation, financial management, and business intelligence into one unified solution.

Nexora enables businesses to manage customer conversations across channels such as WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram while organizing customer information, tracking sales, managing products, and monitoring business performance.

## Key Features

- **Omnichannel Communication:** Centralized management of customer conversations across multiple communication channels.
- **Customer Relationship Management (CRM):** Manage customer profiles, identities, conversations, and customer segments.
- **AI-Powered Automation:** Build intelligent workflows and AI-assisted customer support capabilities.
- **Product & Order Management:** Organize product catalogues, variants, inventory, and sales orders.
- **Financial Management:** Track payments, invoices, expenses, revenue, and financial transactions.
- **Marketing Automation:** Manage campaigns, customer segments, campaign variants, and delivery performance.
- **Business Analytics:** Monitor business performance through centralized dashboards and insights.
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Support multiple businesses with tenant-level data isolation.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Manage users, roles, and permissions securely.

## Architecture

Nexora follows a modern full-stack architecture:

- React frontend for an interactive business dashboard.
- FastAPI backend for business logic and REST API services.
- PostgreSQL for structured data storage.
- SQLAlchemy for database operations and ORM management.
- PostgreSQL Row-Level Security for tenant data isolation.
